plotyy — Plot with Two Y Axes

The plotyy icon on the X,Y Plot tool's gallery row draws the current page with two Y axes: the page's first curve on the left Y axis, and every other curve on a right Y axis with its own range. Two quantities in different units, or of very different sizes, then share one X axis and are both read at full scale.

Syntax

Draw two curves (or more) on one page, then click plotyy:

p = plot(x, y1)       # the left Y axis
plot!(p, x, y2)       # the right Y axis
plot!(p, x, y3)       # also the right Y axis

Description

  • The page's first series is drawn against the left Y axis; the second and every further series against the right one.
  • Each axis is fitted to the curves it carries. The X axis is shared.
  • Every series is drawn as a line, with its colour, width, style, markers and name.
  • The page needs at least two curves: with fewer, the icon says "plotyy needs two curves on this page." and changes nothing.

The plotyy icon

Clicking plotyy re-plots every series of the current page as lines, the first on the left axis and the others on the right, whatever type they were drawn in before. The axes go back to linear and are re-fitted. The icon acts on the page and needs no series selected. The plot icon puts every curve back on the one left axis.

Running the Examples

Paste each example into the iGMT window's Julia console, or into the Julia REPL after using InteractiveGMT, then click plotyy on the gallery row.

Examples

Create Chart with Two Y Axes

Two damped oscillations of very different amplitudes: on one axis the second would be a flat line.

x = range(0, 20; length=1000)
y1 = 200 .* exp.(-0.05 .* x) .* sin.(x)
y2 = 0.8 .* exp.(-0.5 .* x) .* sin.(10 .* x)
p = plot(x, y1; legend="slow")
plot!(p, x, y2; legend="fast")

Plot Two Quantities in Different Units

Temperature (°C) on the left, rainfall (mm) on the right.

x = 1:12
p = plot(x, [8, 9, 12, 14, 17, 21, 24, 24, 21, 17, 12, 9]; legend="temperature", lc=:red, marker=:circle)
plot!(p, x, [110, 90, 80, 70, 50, 20, 10, 10, 30, 80, 100, 120]; legend="rain", lc=:blue, marker=:square)

Put Several Curves on the Right Axis

The first curve is on the left axis, the next two share the right one.

t = range(0, 10; length=300)
p = plot(t, 1000 .* exp.(-t ./ 4); legend="decay")
plot!(p, t, sin.(t); legend="sin", ls=:dash)
plot!(p, t, cos.(t); legend="cos", ls=:dot)

Input Arguments

x — X values, shared by the curves, as in plot.

y1 — the left-axis curve, the page's first series.

y2, y3, … — the right-axis curves, the page's further series.

Keywords

The keywords of plot, for each curve.
